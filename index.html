FAQ

Good to know

Is the instant quote my final price?

Your instant quote is based on your real dimensions and the fence you choose. To lock in your price, upload a short video of the site and a few extra details. We'll send you a firm quote within 1 business day that takes your site conditions into account.

What areas do you service?

We work across the Northern Rivers, from Byron Bay to Pottsville and beyond, including Ballina, Ocean Shores and the surrounding hinterland.

Which fences can I get an instant quote for?

Instant quotes are currently available for:

  • Treated pine paling
  • Lapped & capped treated pine
  • Top cap & exposed posts
  • Colorbond
  • Lapped & capped hardwood
What happens after I get my instant quote?

That's up to you. If the price looks right, upload a short video of the site and a few extra details, and we'll send you a firm quote within 1 business day to lock in your price.
